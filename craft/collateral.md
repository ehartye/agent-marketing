# Collateral that helps someone act

This is the plugin's original craft guidance, informed by [positioning](https://www.aprildunford.com/post/a-quickstart-guide-to-positioning), [tryable launch norms](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html), and [clear campaign next actions](https://howtomarketagame.com/2021/07/12/how-to-market-your-indie-game-a-10-step-plan/). It does not establish that a particular asset generates demand.

Start with the smallest asset that makes the useful outcome credible. A tool may need a runnable example and a before/after screen; a game may need a playable loop and a clip that communicates genre immediately; an educational app may need a specific learner task and a demonstration of the result.

| Asset | Job | Review question |
|---|---|---|
| Landing/product page | Explain audience, outcome, proof and next action | Can an intended person say what they get and try it? |
| Screenshot/demo | Show the real payoff and interaction | Does it depict current behavior at the viewing size? |
| Short native post | Contribute something useful and invite one action | Is it relevant to the community before the link? |
| README/quickstart | Deliver first successful use | Does the exact example run on a clean setup? |
| Press/creator kit | Make accurate coverage easier | Are concise facts, approved images and contact details easy to find? |
| FAQ | Resolve observed objections | Are claims, limitations and availability current? |

Prepare a message spine: audience situation, outcome, proof, differentiator, next action. Adapt it to the channel; do not paste the same generic pitch everywhere. Use actual product footage and verifiable claims. Label concept images and planned features honestly. Quote reactions only with appropriate permission/context; do not turn a child's identity, face or private details into marketing material by default.

Optional sibling plugins can help: agent-vids for captured demonstrations, agent-prose for scripts/voice/instructions, agent-sprites for pixel assets and agent-beeps for audio. Read an available sibling skill only when the chosen deliverable needs it. Agent Marketing still works without them.
