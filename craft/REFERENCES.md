# References

Verified research access: 2026-10-08. Quality indicates source credibility, not applicability to every project. Working papers and practitioner guidance are labeled. Recheck changing policies and metric definitions when planning a real initiative.

- **measurement** — [Google Analytics campaign URL tagging](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10917952?hl=en) (high; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Consistent case-sensitive campaign tags and attributed session reports. Limit: Attribution is not incrementality.
- **ga4-schema** — [Google Analytics Data API schema](https://developers.google.com/analytics/devguides/reporting/data/v1/api-schema) (high; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Distinct metric and dimension definitions. Limit: Property configuration and event scope must be checked.
- **channels** — [Show HN guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) (high; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Substantive tryable maker projects and launch norms. Limit: Rules, not channel performance evidence.
- **reddit** — [Reddit spam policy](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam) (high; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Authentic participation and prohibition of repetitive promotion. Limit: Each community has additional rules.
- **github-traffic** — [GitHub repository traffic REST API](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/metrics/traffic?apiVersion=2026-03-10) (high; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: 14-day UTC traffic collection and permission requirements. Limit: Repository use is not app activation.
- **youtube** — [YouTube Analytics revision history](https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/revision_history) (high; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: View definition changes and reporting delay. Limit: Check report availability and current definitions.
- **hn-api** — [Official Hacker News API](https://github.com/HackerNews/API) (high; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Public item IDs, scores, descendants and thread relationships. Limit: Votes/comments do not measure readers or users.
- **product-hunt** — [Product Hunt launch guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch) (medium; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Maker submissions, launch preparation and no upvote solicitation. Limit: Platform promotional framing does not forecast conversions.
- **experimentation** — [Camuffo et al. — Scientific entrepreneurial decisions, replication](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/smj.3580) (high; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Hypothesis-led entrepreneurial decisions in four trials. Limit: Training intervention, not a family-app channel trial.
- **causality** — [Gordon et al. — Advertising measurement, author working paper (2018)](https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/faculty/gordon_b/files/fb_comparison.pdf) (medium; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Observational and randomized advertising estimates can disagree. Limit: Large Facebook ad experiments; captured working version precedes 2019 publication.
- **sentiment** — [Marinescu et al. — Incentives and online review bias, IZA working paper (2018)](https://docs.iza.org/dp11367.pdf) (medium; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Voluntary review distributions can differ from required or incentivized review samples. Limit: Employer-review and MTurk setting; working version; bias direction is not universal.
- **discovery** — [Osterwalder — Opinions versus facts in customer interviews](https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-one-biggest-error-in-customer-interviews-mistaking-opinions-for-facts) (medium; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Ask about concrete past behavior and existing solutions. Limit: Practitioner guidance, not a controlled result.
- **positioning** — [Dunford — Quickstart guide to positioning](https://www.aprildunford.com/post/a-quickstart-guide-to-positioning) (medium; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Alternatives, distinct capabilities, value, audience and category. Limit: Primarily B2B practitioner cases.
- **player-discovery** — [Zukowski — How to talk to game customers](https://howtomarketagame.com/2020/02/17/how-to-talk-to-your-customers/) (medium; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Player genre and discovery conversations. Limit: Engaged-fan selection bias and dated recruiting mechanics.
- **campaign** — [Zukowski — Indie game marketing plan, 2024 edition](https://howtomarketagame.com/2021/07/12/how-to-market-your-indie-game-a-10-step-plan/) (medium; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Audience/genre research, differentiation and clear next actions. Limit: Mostly Steam; do not adopt universal numeric thresholds.
- **intervals** — [NIST — Confidence intervals for binomial proportions](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc241.htm) (high; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Wilson confidence bounds for proportions. Limit: Independent Bernoulli observations and a fixed analysis design.
- **steam-localization** — [Valve — Localization and languages](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/localization) (high; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Independent store/in-game localization and regional wishlist prioritization. Limit: Platform guidance; language share and wishlists do not establish paid demand.
- **steam-reviews** — [Valve — IUserReviewsService](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/webapi/IUserReviewsService) (high; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Review language, purchase/free flags, playtime, refund status and current interface. Limit: Voluntary reviews do not identify country, income, transaction price or all buyers.
- **steam-pricing** — [Valve — Pricing](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/pricing) (high; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Developer-managed regional prices and pricing tools. Limit: Pricing mechanics, not affordability, willingness to pay or revenue forecasts.
- **gcc-localization** — [Niko Partners and SEF — Speaking the Language (2024)](https://nikopartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Speaking-The-Language_Localization-in-the-MENA-Region_SEF-x-Niko-Partners-Whitepaper_English.pdf) (medium; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: GCC premium purchasing, stated Arabic preference and report-date language supply. Limit: December 2023 survey of 1,225; sponsor interests and limited sample detail; not genre-specific premium PC conversion.
- **mena-country-differences** — [Niko Partners — MENA-3 opportunity summary (2025)](https://nikopartners.com/the-future-of-gaming-in-mena-3-understanding-the-opportunity/) (medium; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Country spending differences and established sports/football engagement. Limit: Vendor market estimates and public survey summary; total ARPU is not premium indie revenue.
- **german-game-market** — [game — Annual Report of the German Games Industry 2026 summary](https://www.game.de/en/publications/annual-report-of-the-german-games-industry-2026/) (medium; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: 2025 German market spending types and PC audience context. Limit: Industry-commissioned research with revised methodology; no German-language genre gap.
- **localization-results** — [Sumo — Academia localization outcomes (2021)](https://www.squeakywheel.ph/blog/localization-for-indie-game-developers) (medium; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Firsthand Thai success and weaker regional outcomes after localization. Limit: One historical before/after game; launch, sales and mods confound attribution; absolute worksheet uncaptured.
- **localization-economics** — [Harris — Indie strategy translation business case (2021)](https://www.positech.co.uk/cliffsblog/2021/01/16/an-indie-strategy-game-translation-business-case-lets-do-the-maths/) (medium; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Include implementation, QA, updates and buyers who would purchase English anyway. Limit: Successor projections and attribution assumptions, not realized localization ROI or current cost quotes.
- **consumer-information** — [Reimers et al. — Imperfect consumer information, working paper (2025)](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/waldfogelreimersriedl.pdf) (high; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Discovery/information friction as an alternative explanation for unmet gamer desire. Limit: 2021 selected enthusiast usage, modeled welfare, working version; FTC hosting is not endorsement; captured tables degraded.
- **tiktok-disclosure** — [TikTok Ads Help: content disclosure setting for creators](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-the-content-disclosure-setting-for-creators) (high; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Posts promoting a brand, product or service must have the content disclosure setting on; legal requirements vary by country and the US FTC Endorsement Guides apply. Limit: Page shows Last updated April 2026; its wording on how the setting is labelled differs from third-party guides; third-party guides describe separate 'Your brand' and 'Branded content' choices that this page does not mention, so confirm the labels in the app before posting.
- **tiktok-video-metrics** — [TikTok Ads Help: video play metrics](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/video-play?lang=en) (high; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Video views count playback starts with replays excluded; average play time includes replays; 'video views at 100%' counts full-length plays. Limit: Ads-side definitions, last updated November 2025; creator-account analytics screens use different terms and may not match; no metric named total play time or completion rate on this page.
- **tiktok-integrity** — [TikTok Integrity and Authenticity policy](https://www.tiktok.com/safety/en/policies-and-engagement/integrity-authenticity) (medium; checked 2026-10-08). Supports: Bulk or automated engagement, bots and trading in artificial engagement are prohibited; manipulative engagement bait can lose For You eligibility. Limit: Page body could not be fetched directly; wording is from search extracts of this page, which was labelled as the May 2024 version; a newer guideline set may exist, so read the live page before relying on exact text.
