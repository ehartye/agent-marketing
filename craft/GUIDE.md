# Evidence-led marketing craft

Marketing helps the right people discover a project, understand its promise, try it and decide whether to come back. The family's scarce resource is often attention and maker time. A plan should make the next decision clearer, rather than generate a long list of places to post.

The guides distinguish published findings, practitioner methods, derived arithmetic and house heuristics. [References](REFERENCES.md) list access dates and limits. [Rules](rules.json) are queryable with `marketing rules <topic>`. A cited source is evidence for a bounded claim, not a guarantee that a campaign works. Verify changing platform rules when planning a real launch.

- [Discovery and viability](discovery.md): distinguish encouragement, use, retention and paid demand.
- [Channels](channels.md): choose an audience and a sustainable distribution test.
- [Competition and positioning](competition.md): research alternatives and explain distinctive value.
- [Campaigns](campaigns.md): connect hypothesis, asset, CTA, measurement and review.
- [Collateral](collateral.md): help people understand, try and explain the project.
- [Measurement](measurement.md): definitions, snapshots, attribution, costs and imports.
- [Reception](reception.md): source context, sentiment review and silent users.
- [Experiments](experiments.md): sample uncertainty and stopping decisions.

The project ledger is evidence you can inspect. It stores no ad-account credentials and performs no outreach. Monitoring reads supported APIs; platform exports and source-linked manual records cover other channels.
