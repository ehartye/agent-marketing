# Demand report format

Use this structure for a saved research deliverable, following the project's naming convention or `docs/research/<topic>-YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Replace field instructions with checked findings; remove unused rows. Keep the decision and comparison readable first, with an audit trail below. Do not fill missing evidence with invented numbers.

## Decision and scope

State the recommended next action, whether the opportunity is established or only a hypothesis, and what could reverse the decision. Name the as-of date, game/product scope, feasible maker constraints and provisional assumptions. A finding of insufficient evidence is a valid result.

## Comparable buyer cohorts

| Cohort: language, geography/access, platform, genre, constraint, price | Observed purchasing evidence | Unmet preference and current substitutes | Reach/channel evidence | Counterevidence and unknowns | Decision/status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Populate with sourced evidence or explicitly labeled hypotheses | Include sample/data period; separate premium and free-to-play | Verify actual support/features; absent documentation stays unknown | Checked route to these buyers; reach is not acquisition proof | Include the strongest alternative explanation | Probe / partner first / defer, with reason |

Do not use an automatic numeric rank unless the owner explicitly chose a documented decision rubric. Explain why the evidence favors the next probe.

## Delivery and economics

| Option | Time now and later | Risk: likelihood and consequence | Complexity | Best practice | Maintenance and costs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Compare the serious candidates | Native QA/recruiting/build estimates and lag; label estimates | What could invalidate demand, access or execution | Localization/UI/payment/channel requirements | State the relevant established practice and source | Updates, support, partner obligations and net break-even assumptions |

Separate translation-only incremental economics from the cost of building a new product. Show absolute amounts and denominators if available, currencies, price/discount/refund assumptions, and sensitivities. Unknown reachable audience or paid conversion means a revenue forecast is not established.

## Evidence audit

| Claim with citation | Primary source and checked date | Publication and data dates | Sample/denominator and measurement | Credibility/relevance with reasons | Limit and inference status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cite the exact supporting page or report near the claim | Author, sponsor, URL; inspected text/chart/abstract | Do not substitute crawl date for fieldwork | Language, country, genre and platform coverage | These are separate judgments | Observed / estimate / anecdote / hypothesis; state counterevidence |

## Search coverage

Record search terms in each searched language, sources, inclusion/exclusion rules, time window, candidate/record counts and inaccessible evidence. Explain selection bias and geographic/language gaps. If only English discovery was performed, say so; native research remains an explicit next step.

## One falsifiable next test

Name the hypothesis, eligible buyers, unit/sample/recruiting rules, actual deliverable and price, acquisition/exposure, primary outcome and refund/return window, time/spend cap, owner, decision date, and continue/change/stop rules. Tie the decision threshold to the project's economics or a stated qualitative gate, not a universal wishlist or conversion benchmark. Describe how alternative explanations will be handled. Mark unrun experiments as planned.

## Evidence retention

Link the report from the project brief. Where a project ledger exists, store verified alternatives in `competitors` and bounded supporting/contradictory claims in `evidence`; keep hypotheses labeled and do not fabricate observations. Keep sensitive interview notes local. Authorized wiki capture/ingestion should preserve primary evidence and a synthesis; report unresolved capture issues. Record only actions actually performed.
