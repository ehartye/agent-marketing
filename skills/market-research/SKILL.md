# Research alternatives and positioning

Read [competition craft](../../craft/competition.md) and [CLI contract](../../docs/CLI.md). Use market-setup. Check existing project/wiki evidence before browsing. Search the task and customer vocabulary, genre and workarounds, not just the project's name.

Use current official product pages, repositories, store listings, changelogs and firsthand demos. Record search terms and coverage. Compare direct alternatives, substitutes and doing nothing. Capture dated claims about audience, outcome, features, access/price, proof and friction with exact source URLs. Unknown features stay unknown; a missing claim is not proof of absence. Marketing prior-art research does not establish legal freedom to operate.

Write `competitors` records with only verified claims and `evidence` records for relevant market observations. Include contradictory evidence. Explain differences as a positioning hypothesis, not a uniqueness certificate or revenue forecast. For AI-assisted projects, connect actual benefits to customer value; development method alone is not a differentiator.

Deliver a concise sourced comparison and a suggested audience/situation/outcome/proof positioning statement, with the cheapest test that could refute it. If wiki discovery/ingestion is authorized, capture new evidence and record the synthesis there using wiki-master; otherwise keep the cited brief in the project.

Finish when every material comparison has a checked date/source and the next positioning test can be evaluated.
