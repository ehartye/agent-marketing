# Research demand, alternatives and positioning

Read [competition craft](../../craft/competition.md) and [CLI contract](../../docs/CLI.md). Use market-setup. Check existing project/wiki evidence before browsing. Search the task and customer vocabulary, genre and workarounds, not just the project's name.

For demand, underserved populations or language-market questions, also read [demand methodology](../../craft/demand.md) and use the [demand report format](../../library/demand-report.md). Deliver a saved, readable report with a recommendation, comparable cohort table, dated claim/source table, counterevidence, uncertainty and one falsifiable next test. Regional wealth, language share and total gaming spend cannot establish unmet paid demand. A report may conclude that no candidate is established.

For a market-wide question (what is doing well or badly, which of the owner's games or concepts to place first), use the [market landscape format](../../library/market-landscape-report.md) and the landscape rules in the demand methodology. Build the candidate list from the owner's wiki and repository list (`gh repo list`) together, because neither is complete, and read each repository's README and recent activity before classifying it. Where the owner can build cheaply, include concepts to build, tested by a staged funnel with a cap and stop rules.

A fetch tool that summarizes a page returns a model's reading of it, not the page. Treat those figures as leads. For any number that drives the decision, capture the page verbatim through wiki-master (its clip tools render pages and record failures) and check the number against the capture; otherwise label the claim as extracted. If a page will not load, say so and downgrade the claim; never promote a search snippet. When research is split into parallel passes by lens, give every pass the same claim record: the claim with number, denominator and unit; URL; author; publication date; data period; access date; method and sample; credibility and relevance judged separately; and status (observed, modeled-estimate, documented-rule, anecdote or hypothesis).

Use current official product pages, repositories, store listings, changelogs, original research and firsthand demos. Record search terms, inclusion rules, language/geographic coverage and inaccessible sources. Check publication and data dates separately from access dates. Grade source credibility and relevance separately; inspect methods, sample, sponsor and measurement before using a number. Follow a summary to its primary source when available. Label facts, estimates, anecdotes and your hypotheses; show contradictory evidence. Unknown features stay unknown; a missing claim is not proof of absence. Marketing prior-art research does not establish legal freedom to operate.

For an existing project, write `competitors` records with only verified claims and `evidence` records for relevant market observations, including contradictions. Preserve sample and transfer limits in each claim; use `hypothesis` for an untested opportunity. For an exploratory question without a project, save the report first; do not invent a project budget or results to satisfy ledger fields. Explain differences as a positioning hypothesis, not a uniqueness certificate or revenue forecast. For AI-assisted projects, connect actual benefits to customer value; development method alone is not a differentiator.

Deliver a concise sourced comparison and a suggested audience/situation/outcome/proof positioning statement, with the cheapest test that could refute it. Save the report so the owner can read it in the campaign desk with `marketing document save report`; it appears under Research fit. Keep the Markdown source in the owner's wiki (a synthesis for reusable outside knowledge, project notes or decisions for the owner's own choices) using wiki-master, and never in this plugin's `docs/research`, which ships to every install. Record the claims that drive the decision as `evidence` with their audit fields. If wiki capture is not authorized, keep the cited report in the workspace.

Finish when material claims have checked sources and scope limits, the report distinguishes paid demand from proxies, options explain time/risk/complexity/best practice/maintenance, and the next demand or positioning test has a cap, decision date and continue/change/stop rule. Validate any ledger writes. Do not spend, publish, contact people or run a paid test without the owner's instruction.
