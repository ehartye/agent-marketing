# Turn results into a decision

Read [measurement](../../craft/measurement.md), [reception](../../craft/reception.md), [discovery](../../craft/discovery.md) and [CLI contract](../../docs/CLI.md). Use market-setup. Run `marketing report --project <id>` and `marketing advise <id>`; inspect the cited observation/reaction/evidence records before accepting automated suggestions.

Separate attention, attributed first use, returning cohorts and payment. Preserve metric definitions and periods. Do not infer unique people across platforms or causal incrementality from tagged links. Count reviewed captured sentiment separately from unreviewed suggestions; explain sampling and silent-user gaps. The CLI's 20% activation trigger is a house triage heuristic, not a market benchmark.

Choose the most consequential bottleneck or evidence gap. Explain the observed facts, your interpretation, plausible alternatives, and what would contradict it. Address time/spend already invested, sustainable workload and uncertainty. Small counts should be shown directly; calculate experiment uncertainty with market-experiment when appropriate. A single returner or favorable comment does not establish viability.

Deliver one prioritized next test with a source-linked rationale, an owner, a time/spend cap, a prespecified outcome, and a stop/review condition. Preserve failed and contradictory results. Record planned follow-up in the ledger when requested; do not turn the review into unsolicited outreach or spending.

Finish when the owner can tell what was measured, what remains unknown, and exactly which observation will change the next decision.
